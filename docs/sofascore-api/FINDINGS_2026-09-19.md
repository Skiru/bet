# 2026-09-19 — the coupon was 88.8% OVER, and that was the model, not the board

Found while reviewing all 520 positions of the 2026-09-19 coupon, position by
position. Three defects, all fixed; two residuals left on purpose, with the
evidence for leaving them.

## F54 — a symmetric normal on a right-skewed count

`calc_p_central_raw` integrates a normal CDF. A count is right-skewed, so its
median sits below its mean, and a bell curve centred on the mean puts too much
mass above a line near the centre — which is where almost every posted line
sits. The error is one-sided: every OVER rung inflated, every UNDER rung
deflated by the same amount.

Measured three independent ways, all agreeing:

1. **Against each position's own sample** (2026-09-19 coupon, 476 checkable
   positions): OVER claimed `p_central` 0.4371 where the positions' own last-20
   records said 0.3465 — **+9.1 pp**. UNDER: 0.5320 against 0.5462, −1.4 pp.
2. **Against 1,868,474 settled rows** replayed out of the cache: OVER hit
   42.69%, UNDER 57.31%. The one live settled day reproduces it: 42.42% /
   57.96%. Not a one-day artifact.
3. **Mechanism**: the samples are right-skewed (mean − median = +0.248, 71.2%
   positive), and the per-family overstatement is monotone in how skewed the
   family is — `goals_1h_for` +19.2 pp, `goals_2h_for` +17.9 pp, down to
   `goals_2h_total` +6.7 pp.

On the one day with settled prices this cost, by family: `goals_1h_for` OVER
predicted 38.9% and hit 25.6% (−47% ROI); `corners_1h_total` OVER −41%;
`goals_for` OVER −34%. Those were the largest families in the coupon.

**Fix**: price counts from a discrete negative binomial with the same mean and
predictive variance (`NEGATIVE_BINOMIAL_METRICS`, `nb_survival`). Being
discrete it also needs no support floor — the atom at zero is an atom, not a
sliver of a bell curve reaching into negative counts.

Replayed over the same 1,868,474 rows:

| estimator | Brier | OVER bias |
|---|---|---|
| normal (was) | 0.17278 | +0.0469 |
| negative binomial | 0.16865 | −0.0055 |

It is **not** applied everywhere. Chosen per metric at n>=2000 and validated on
an event-id split half: it wins on twelve metrics and loses on the high-mean
shot and foul families, which are near enough symmetric that added skew is a
cost. A metric not on the list keeps the normal, so an unmeasured metric cannot
change behaviour by being added upstream. The per-half variants carry only
190–352 settled rows each, below that bar, and are listed on their own
measurement from the live settled day, where every one with n>=150 improved —
`goals_1h_for` most of all at −0.03612.

Live effect on the whole 2026-09-19 sheet (not just selected rows, which are
subject to a winner's curse):

| | OVER gap | UNDER gap |
|---|---|---|
| before | +0.0474 | −0.0474 |
| after | −0.0039 | +0.0039 |

## F55 — a rung quoted on one side only was selected by the model alone

With only one side quoted there is nothing to devig, so `market_p` is None,
`bar_probability` falls back to the raw model at w=1, and `bar_is_unreachable`
returns False without testing anything. 71 of 520 rows, at a median surplus of
4.95 against 0.24 for anchored rows and median odds 12.0 against 3.10. That is
the signature of a missing check, not an edge.

**Fix**: `run_sheet` withholds VALUE when `market_p` is None, the same rule and
the same reason as the `NO_LADDER_CHECK` gate beside it.

## F56 — the same hole on the derived path

`both_over_*` and `handicap_*` are built in `derived.py`, which has its own
verdict block. Its equivalent check is `p_marginal` — the joint priced off the
market's own per-side marginals with our measured dependence. When those
marginals are not quoted at that line `p_marginal` is None, and the check was
**skipped rather than failed**, so the row became VALUE unchecked. 23 rows, at
a median price of 12.0, every one also carrying `ONE_SIDED_LADDER`, and with no
per-side sample in the artifacts that could have checked them either.

**Fix**: None is a refusal, not a pass. The module docstring already said these
must be "a LEAN with the reason recorded, never a VALUE"; the branch was simply
never written.

## Residuals, left on purpose

- **`corners_2h_for` still runs +0.073 on OVER** (n=239 on the sheet). It is
  not in the NB set because its 56 settled rows said NB was *worse* there. 56
  rows decides nothing in either direction, and its siblings `corners_for` and
  `corners_1h_for` both improved under NB. Left alone rather than fitted to
  n=56. Revisit when the settled table carries per-half rows in volume.
- **The extreme tail still disagrees with the price by multiples.** `corners_for`
  0.5 UNDER — a team taking zero corners — prices at p_bar 0.044 against a
  devigged market 0.002, a 20x disagreement at odds of 39. Per §"price gap
  annotates, ladder gap demotes" a price gap is deliberately not a demotion, so
  no gate fires. Shipping a new gate here would be an unmeasured rule; recorded
  instead.
- **`K_PRICE` and `MAX_LADDER_SIGMA` remain NOT_FITTED**, so every row still
  carries `UNFITTED_CONSTANTS`. `MAX_LADDER_SIGMA` cannot fit until more days
  settle against a live ladder — backfilled rows have `market_p` NULL by
  construction.
