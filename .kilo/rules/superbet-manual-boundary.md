# Superbet Manual Boundary

Never scrape or automate bookmaker interaction. The sole production operator workflow is manual Superbet Bet Builder.

All analytical candidates remain conditional until a human verifies the exact market/line and enters a real visible Superbet quote. Missing quotes do not block analysis, but they block EV, Kelly/stakes, bettable status, and an executable coupon. S9 is human-only; synthetic or agent-generated approval is invalid.
