# Basketball - context checklist and sources

Checked 2026-10-26 (`docs/sofa/history/analitycy_2026-10-26/sources_measured_sports.md`).
Access and evidence legends as in `sources-hockey.md`.

## Mandatory - every match, in this order

1. **Is it on** - postponement, venue.
2. **Availability** - NBA: the official injury report (teams file by 17:00
   local the day before, update 11:00-13:00 local on game day; snapshots
   every 15 min) and Rotowire's lineups; EuroLeague: BasketNews' daily
   injury report. Every **player prop**: status (Out / Doubtful /
   Questionable / GTD), a minutes restriction, a starting change.
3. **Rest** - back-to-back (dossier `rest_days`, `games_7d`), a EuroLeague
   double week, a domestic game wedged between EuroLeague games.
4. **Stakes** - only when they act through who plays (tanking, a decided
   seed, a domestic game for a EuroLeague side).

## Checklist

| item | moves | evidence | carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Star / starter Out or Doubtful** since the price | team total ↓, handicap moves; teammates' usage props ↑ | A (size by player) | WATCH through the team's line; NO_BET on a prop whose player is Out on two domains (the official report + one), else WATCH |
| **Minutes restriction** (return from injury, typically 15-28 min) | that player's props ↓ | A | WATCH on his OVER props (NO_BET only on two domains) |
| **Starting-lineup change** | the promoted player's props ↑, the demoted ↓ | A | WATCH the demoted player's OVER |
| **Back-to-back, second night** | tired side ~2.3 pts ↓ (≥ 3 days' rest vs B2B) | E (Small; Abolins et al.) | WATCH on legs needing the tired side to score / cover |
| **Load management** (often the B2B second night) | as Out | F | WATCH when announced |
| **Travel west / time zones** | away side ↓ | E- | note |
| **Altitude** (Denver, Utah) | home side ↑ (~66-67% vs an equal team) | E | note (priced) |
| **EuroLeague double week; domestic game for a EuroLeague side** | rotation in the domestic game → that side's total / handicap ↓ | E- / F | WATCH through who plays |
| **Tanking / nothing to play for** | blowouts, stars sit | E- | through availability |
| **Garbage time** (expected blowout) | starters' props ↓; a big favourite's handicap ↓ (bench gives points back) | F | WATCH a starter's OVER in a lopsided matchup |
| **Referee crew** (NBA, ~09:00 ET game day) | free throws / total ↑ ↓ by crew | E- (not shown to beat the line) | note |
| **Overtime** | full-game totals include OT; halves / quarters never (code scope) | A | a scope question, not context |
| **Asian / Australian leagues: an import missing** | team total ↓ | F | WATCH |
| **Season opener, a trade's debut, coach change** | var ↑ | F | note |
